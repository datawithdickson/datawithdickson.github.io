01 / About

Data is only useful when someone acts on it.

I am a Data Analyst who works across the full analysis workflow: querying data with SQL, cleaning and exploring it in Python, modelling it in Excel, and presenting it in Power BI dashboards.

I care most about data storytelling. A correct number that nobody understands changes nothing, so I focus on framing the business question first, being explicit about assumptions, and ending every analysis with a clear recommendation a non-technical stakeholder can act on.

I treat correlation with caution, flag data quality issues early, and prefer a simple, defensible answer over a complicated one.

Problem solving

Start from the business question, then choose the method.

Data storytelling

Answer first, evidence second, action items last.

Quality first

Validate, document assumptions, flag outliers and bias.
