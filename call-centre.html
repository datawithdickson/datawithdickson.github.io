PROJECT 03 / CLAUDE AI · PYTHON · HTML · CSS · JAVASCRIPT

PwC Call Centre Trends

An AI-assisted analysis of 5,000 PwC call centre records, exploring operational performance, customer satisfaction and call resolution through an interactive dashboard. The project uses Claude to support analysis and dashboard development, with metric definitions and reported figures documented for review.

CSAT (ratings of 4 or 5)

The analysis also found a 72.9% resolution rate across all incoming calls and an average speed of answer of 67.5 seconds. Differences between agents in unanswered rate, resolution, speed of answer and satisfaction were not statistically significant. Speed of answer showed no linear relationship with satisfaction.

What the project covers

  • Call volume and hourly operating patterns.
  • Answered and unanswered calls.
  • Resolution rates for answered calls and all incoming calls.
  • Customer satisfaction ratings and average rating.
  • Speed of answer, talk time and agent-level comparisons.
